Home Extensions in Derby

A home extension is one of the most popular home improvements for Derby homeowners. With property prices in Derby continuing to rise, many families find it more cost-effective to extend their existing home than to move to a larger property. A well-designed extension can add significant space and value — creating an open-plan kitchen-diner, a ground-floor bedroom, a home office, or a utility room.

We manage the full process from planning advice through to final decoration. Our team handles structural design, groundworks, brickwork, roofing, windows, plastering, flooring, and all finishing work. You deal with one team throughout, which keeps the project coordinated and communication clear.

Loft Conversions in Derby

Many Derby properties — particularly the Victorian and Edwardian terraces found throughout the city — have loft spaces that are either unused or used only for storage. A loft conversion is one of the most cost-effective ways to add a bedroom, bathroom, or home office without reducing garden space or altering the property's footprint.

We specialise in dormer loft conversions, Velux conversions, and hip-to-gable conversions. We assess the existing roof structure, advise on the most suitable conversion type, and manage all aspects of the project from structural work and insulation through to staircase installation and final decoration.

Bathroom and Kitchen Fitting in Derby

A new kitchen or bathroom can transform the feel of a home and significantly increase its value. We approach both as complete room transformations rather than simple installations. That means paying attention to the structural preparation, the layout, the quality of tiling and plastering, and the fine details of finishing that make the difference between an ordinary result and an exceptional one.

We can work with client-supplied units and fittings, or advise on suppliers and designs that suit the space and budget. Either way, our goal is a finished room that looks sharp and performs well in daily use.
