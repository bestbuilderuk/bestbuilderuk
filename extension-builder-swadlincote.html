Types of Home Extension We Build

  • Single-Storey Rear Extensions — the most popular extension type in Swadlincote, ideal for enlarging a kitchen-diner or creating an open-plan living space
  • Double-Storey Extensions — add space on two levels simultaneously, typically adding a bedroom above an extended kitchen or living room
  • Side Return Extensions — utilise the narrow side passage of a terraced or semi-detached property to add width to the ground floor
  • Wrap-Around Extensions — combine a rear and side extension for maximum ground-floor space
  • Orangeries and Garden Rooms — light-filled extensions that bring the garden into the home
  • Over-Garage Extensions — adding a room above an existing integral or attached garage

The Extension Process: What to Expect

Building a home extension involves several distinct stages, and understanding the process helps homeowners plan effectively. Here is how we typically approach an extension project in Swadlincote:

1. Initial consultation and site visit. We visit the property, discuss your requirements and budget, and assess the structural and planning context. This is completely free and carries no obligation.

2. Design and planning advice. We advise on whether the project requires planning permission or falls within permitted development rights. Where planning permission is needed, we can help prepare drawings and manage the application.

3. Building regulations. All extensions require building regulations approval. We manage this process and ensure the project meets all structural, thermal, and fire safety requirements.

4. Construction. We carry out all groundworks, brickwork, roofing, windows, internal plastering, flooring, and finishing. You deal with one team throughout — no subcontracting to unknown trades.

5. Snagging and handover. Before we consider the project complete, we carry out a thorough snagging process to address any minor issues and ensure the finished extension meets our standards.

How Much Does a Home Extension Cost in Swadlincote?

Extension costs vary significantly depending on size, specification, and complexity. As a rough guide:

  • A small single-storey rear extension (up to 15m²): from approximately £20,000–£28,000
  • A medium single-storey extension (15–30m²): from approximately £28,000–£45,000
  • A double-storey extension: from approximately £40,000–£75,000+

These are indicative figures only. The best way to get an accurate cost for your project is to call us for a free site visit and itemised quote.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a home extension cost in Swadlincote?

The cost of a home extension in Swadlincote depends on the size, specification, and complexity of the project. A single-storey rear extension typically starts from around £20,000–£35,000. We provide free, itemised quotes so you know exactly what is included.

Do I need planning permission for a home extension in Swadlincote?

Many home extensions in Swadlincote fall within permitted development rights and do not require planning permission. However, this depends on the size, position, and type of extension. We can advise on whether your project needs planning permission before any work begins.

How long does a home extension take to build?

A typical single-storey extension takes 8–14 weeks from groundworks to completion, depending on size and specification. Larger or more complex projects may take longer. We provide a realistic programme at the start of every project.

Can you manage the full extension project including planning?

Yes. We can advise on planning requirements, help prepare drawings, manage the building regulations process, and deliver the full construction from groundworks to final decoration.
