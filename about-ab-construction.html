About AB Construction UK Ltd

AB Construction UK Ltd (registered as AB Construction U.K. Ltd., Company No. 14328530) are professional builders based in Woodville, Swadlincote, Derbyshire. Founded in 2022, the company has quickly established itself as one of the most trusted building contractors in South Derbyshire, completing hundreds of projects for homeowners across the region.

Who We Are

AB Construction UK Ltd are a fully insured, locally based building company serving Swadlincote and the wider South Derbyshire area. The company is led by experienced tradespeople with over 15 years of combined experience in the construction industry. They are known for transparent pricing, quality craftsmanship, and a professional approach to every project — from small brickwork repairs to full double-storey extensions.

What We Do

AB Construction UK Ltd specialise in the following building services across Swadlincote and South Derbyshire:

  • Home Extensions — Single and double storey extensions, side returns, and wraparound extensions
  • Loft Conversions — Velux, dormer, hip-to-gable, and mansard loft conversions
  • Kitchen Fitting — Complete kitchen design, supply, and installation
  • Bathroom Installation — Full bathrooms, wet rooms, en-suites, and luxury bathrooms
  • Brickwork & Masonry — Bricklaying, repointing, stonework, and blockwork
  • Bespoke Joinery — Staircases, doors, windows, and fitted furniture
  • Garage Conversions — Converting garages into living space, offices, or playrooms
  • Full Property Renovations — Complete refurbishment and remodelling projects
